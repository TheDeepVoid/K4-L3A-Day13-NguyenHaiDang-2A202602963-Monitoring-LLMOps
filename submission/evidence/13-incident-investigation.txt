INVESTIGATION - python scripts/investigate.py --latency-threshold 2000 --top 2
==========================================================================
Why 2000 ms and not the SLO's 3000 ms: this log deliberately contains both
the healthy phase and the incident phase. With 20 samples of which 10 are
healthy, the nearest-rank p95 lands on 2651 ms, so the SLO's good_event
(<= 3000 ms) is NOT breached. That is a real property of averaging a mixed
window, and it is why an investigation does not start from the SLO: the
symptom to look for is '10x slower than baseline', not 'over 3000 ms'.
